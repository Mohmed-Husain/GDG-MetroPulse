# Demand Anchors and Calibration Notes: Gujarat Metro (Ahmedabad–Gandhinagar)

## 1. Reported Ridership Progression
- **System Scope**: As of 2026, the network spans 67.56 km with 54 operational stations across two main corridors: North-South (Red Line APMC–Motera, extended by Yellow Line to Gandhinagar Mahatma Mandir) and East-West (Blue Line Thaltej Gam–Vastral Gam, plus GNLU–GIFT City spur).
- **Monthly Trajectory**:
  - 2023: 12 to 27 lakh passengers/month.
  - 2024: 27 to 35 lakh passengers/month.
  - Late 2025 – 2026: Crossed 44 lakh passengers/month (~1.5 lakh daily average).
  - *Source*: The Hindu (Jan 18, 2026) [Link](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/gujarat/ahmedabadgandhinagar-metro-ridership-rises-fourfold-in-three-years/article70519063.ece) & Times of India (Jan 07, 2026) [Link](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/ahmedabad-metro-ridership-up-earnings-stall/articleshow/126359757.cms).

## 2. Temporal Demand Profile & Peak Hours
- **Weekday Peaks**:
  - Morning Peak: 08:30 – 10:30 (Heavy office rush towards Old High Court CBD, Gandhinagar Sachivalaya, and Infocity).
  - Evening Peak: 17:30 – 20:00 (Reverse commuter flow + university students returning from Knowledge Corridor).
- **Weekend / Holiday Profile**:
  - Flatter midday profile with strong evening surge (17:00 – 21:30) concentrated around leisure hubs: Sabarmati Riverfront (Old High Court), Kankaria Lakefront (East-West), and Gandhinagar Akshardham.
- **Directional Imbalance**:
  - In morning peak: ~65% flow is southbound into Ahmedabad CBD (Old High Court) and northbound into Gandhinagar IT/Secretariat (Infocity/Sachivalaya).
  - Evening peak mirrors this flow.

## 3. Ticketing Share & Gate Data
- **Ticketing Media Split**:
  - **Paper QR Tickets**: ~45-50% (Purchased at TVMs and station ticket windows; causes queuing bottlenecks during events).
  - **Mobile App QR (Ahmedabad Metro App / WhatsApp)**: ~20-25%.
  - **Contactless Smart Cards & RuPay NCMC**: ~25-30% (Used primarily by daily office commuters and students).
- **Data Availability**:
  - QR tickets have explicit Origin-Destination (OD) recorded at purchase.
  - Smart Cards / NCMC record Tap-In station and timestamp at entry; destination is confirmed only at Tap-Out.
  - Real-time gate counters provide instantaneous passenger inflows per station every 30 seconds.

## 4. Known Operational Bottlenecks & Problem Stations
1. **Old High Court (STN_07)**:
   - Primary two-level interchange station connecting North-South and East-West corridors. Transfer concourse suffers cross-traffic friction during peak transfer intervals (every 10-12 minutes).
2. **Motera Stadium (STN_15)**:
   - Surge factor of 500% to 800% within a 60-minute window post-cricket matches / concerts. Platform clearing requires pre-event stabling of empty rakes at AEC/Sabarmati sidings and manual pulsing of fare gates.
3. **Sabarmati Multimodal Hub (STN_12)**:
   - Growing transfer volume as Indian Railways and High Speed Rail (HSR Bullet Train) integration progresses.
4. **Infocity (STN_27) & Sachivalaya (STN_30)**:
   - High concentration of morning alightings within a narrow 45-minute window (09:15 – 10:00), creating stairwell and escalator congestion.
